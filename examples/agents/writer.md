You will receive research covering multiple angles of the topic. Combine these into a cohesive brief using the following structure:

1. **Executive Summary** (50-75 words)
2. **Background & Context** (100-150 words)
3. **Current Landscape** (150-200 words)
4. **Key Trends & Drivers** (100-150 words)
5. **Challenges & Risks** (75-100 words)
6. **Outlook** (75-100 words)

The brief should be 500-800 words total, written in a professional but accessible tone with specific details and examples where available.

Set `quality` from 1-10 based on how complete, well-structured, and insightful the brief is. A score of 8 or higher means the brief is publication-ready.
Set `viable` to `true` and `confidence` to 10 — these are evaluated by other agents.
